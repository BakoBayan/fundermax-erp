# Style Rule: No Sparkle Emojis

1. **Sparkle Emojis Prohibition**:
   - Do NOT add any sparkle emojis (`✨`, `❇️`, `✳️`, `🌟`, `💫`, etc.) anywhere in the codebase, database, documentation, UI, or print slips.
2. **Icon Prohibition**:
   - Do NOT import or use `Sparkles`, `Sparkle`, or glitter icons from `lucide-react` or SVG assets.
3. **Approved Styling**:
   - Maintain a crisp, professional, ink-friendly aesthetic. Use domain-specific symbols like `🪵` (Wood Panels) and `📦` (Customer Orders) or standard utilitarian Lucide icons (`Layers`, `PackageCheck`, `Printer`).
